The score

Our own BPM, rebuilt from box scores with Basketball-Reference's BPM 2.0 formula. It matches their published BPM at r = 0.995 across 25 seasons. Small samples are shrunk, then ranked within the season among players with 500+ minutes. A 75 is the top quarter of the league.

The models

Trained on every season from 2001-02 to 2025-26, each paired with the player's next season. Inputs were cut from 52 candidates by keeping only those that improved predictions on seasons the model had not seen.

Read it as a shortlist

These are odds, not promises. The models cannot see injuries, coaching changes or crowded rotations, and the projected score carries a wide range. Use the board to pick who to watch on film.
